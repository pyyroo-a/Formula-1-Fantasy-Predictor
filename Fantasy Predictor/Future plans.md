# Future plans

The bigger picture PitWall sits in. The north star: **build a career in the F1
industry.** Everything here is picked to move towards that, not just to build cool
things. Edit freely, this is meant to grow.

## The framing

I probably won't get "noticed by an F1 team" directly, that's rare and mostly luck.
What I CAN work towards is getting noticed by the F1 data community and the people who
hire into it: FastF1's maintainers, the F1 data people on X and Reddit, engineers at
teams and their tech partners (AWS, timing and strategy suppliers, analytics companies).

That community is small, public and reachable, and reputation there is what turns into
a job. So every project should point at that audience.

Consistency beats one flashy drop. A steady public track record is what hiring managers
read. Being honest about results, like PitWall's backtest where the simple baseline
beat my model, is a feature to talk about, not a weakness.

## Three threads, one arc

1. **Contribute to FastF1.** Highest signal, lowest competition. Almost everyone doing
   public F1 data work uses it, so a merged PR is a credential people recognise. Start
   with docs fixes and "good first issue" tickets, ideally papercuts I've hit myself in
   PitWall (the `enable_cache` crash when the cache folder doesn't exist is that kind of
   thing). With the October 2026 login wall, there's probably more to help with now.
2. **A race strategy project.** Fantasy is fun but it's not what teams do. Strategy is
   a real engineering job: tyre degradation per compound and circuit, pit windows,
   undercut and overcut, safety car what ifs. That's the separate F1 Strategy Lab
   project (its README is the source of truth). Other parked ideas: driver vs driver
   corner telemetry, a quali gap and track evolution analyser, a live race dashboard.
3. **Document and post.** A short write up per milestone: what I built, the honest
   evaluation, what failed. Post on LinkedIn regularly.

## PitWall's role

My polished, deployed showpiece: the proof I can ship and look after a real full
stack ML app. Not the flagship, but the credibility anchor. Keep it low maintenance
and looking finished.

## Order

1. FastF1: one small PR.
2. The strategy project, built bit by bit.
3. Write about both, regularly.
4. PitWall: keep it polished (the [Roadmap](Roadmap.md)).

## Career reference

- Target roles: data engineer, ML engineer, race strategist, simulation engineer,
  performance analyst, software engineer.
- Where they are: F1 teams plus tech partners (AWS, timing and strategy suppliers,
  sports analytics firms).
- Community: FastF1 GitHub and Discord, F1 data X, r/F1Technical, r/formula1.
