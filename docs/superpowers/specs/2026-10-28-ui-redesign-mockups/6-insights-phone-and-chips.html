How we worked this out

Every number comes from the game's own official 2026 points, saved after each race. Nothing here is a prediction.

3x: the top scoring driver's points that race, which is the extra a 3x adds over a 2x. Limitless: the best team with no budget cap minus the best $100M team, with that round's prices. No Negative: 7 times the average points lost below zero, over every driver and constructor.

The catch: it's one season so far. We can't check yet if a track that was good for a chip stays good the next year. Once there's a second season of official points, that check gets done and shows up here.
