The idea: you put your real teams in once, and everything else just reads them.

  • One tab, "My real teams", replaces Planner and Chip Advisor. Pick your team at the top, and both answers show for it at once. No more typing your team in twice. (Your 3 saved planner teams carry over.)
  • The transfer ladder becomes steps: taller means more points, red is the highest. Each step lists exactly what goes out and comes in, and why, from practice.
  • Plain labels: "Free transfers this week: 2" instead of "2 (free)", and "1 transfer, free" or "a 3rd would cost 10 points".
  • "Edit this team" opens the same picker as Manual Team (drivers by team, constructors A to Z), with the right constructor names. That fixes the Racing Bulls and Haas scoring 0 bug for good.
  • Same server answers as now, nothing about the maths changes. It just asks for the ladder and the chips together.
